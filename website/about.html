About

Background

Before my PhD, I worked on machine learning for gravitational-wave astronomy at ICTS-TIFR in Bengaluru, training neural networks to remove noise from detector data, and wrote my Master's thesis on physics-informed neural networks for modified gravity. At Northumbria I'm part of the S3Park group.

Outside research

Away from the desk I photograph the night sky, hike, and cycle.
