S³PARK

Luca Franci's group at Northumbria University studies how energy moves across scales and is dissipated in collisionless turbulent plasmas, and how particles gain energy along the way. We look at turbulence, magnetic reconnection, kinetic instabilities and wave–particle interactions, combining supercomputer simulations with measurements from spacecraft such as Solar Orbiter, Parker Solar Probe and MMS.

The group also runs the S³PARK workshop on spacecraft observations and simulations of turbulent plasmas. The first was held at Northumbria in September 2026.

Luca Franci

Group leader, Royal Society University Research Fellow and Assistant Professor

Luca leads the group and supervises my PhD. He studies kinetic plasma turbulence with high-resolution hybrid simulations, and has used them to model spacecraft measurements such as those from MMS at Earth's magnetopause. He chaired the first S³PARK workshop.

Zubair Shaikh

Postdoctoral researcher

Zubair is a space plasma physicist working on turbulence, plasma waves, coherent structures and particle dynamics in the solar wind and Earth's magnetosphere. Before joining Northumbria he worked at the University of Texas at Dallas and at the Space Sciences Laboratory at UC Berkeley, after a PhD at the Indian Institute of Geomagnetism.

Pranab Deka

Postdoctoral researcher, joining from KU Leuven

Pranab is a computational plasma physicist at the Centre for mathematical Plasma Astrophysics at KU Leuven, working on particle-in-cell and MHD simulations, cosmic rays and numerical methods. For his PhD in applied mathematics at the University of Innsbruck he developed exponential time integrators for the MHD equations and for the diffusion of Galactic cosmic rays.
