Projects

Earlier research and study projects.

Modified gravity with neural networks

Master's thesis at IIT Bombay with S. Shankaranarayanan, 2023–2024

I used physics-informed neural networks, built with NeuroDiffEq, to solve Einstein's equations in modified theories of gravity. Studying spherically symmetric vacuum solutions, I found signs that classical results such as Birkhoff's theorem and the no-hair theorem can fail outside general relativity, and found a non-singular, Schwarzschild-like black hole solution in Stelle gravity. Work continues on what its signatures in gravitational-wave data would be.

Detecting coronal holes with a neural network

ARIES, Nainital, with Dipankar Banerjee, 2023

I implemented a U-Net, a convolutional neural network, to pick out coronal holes in SDO/AIA 193 Å images and HMI magnetograms, and evaluated it with intersection over union and binary cross-entropy.

Jets from active galactic nuclei

IIT Indore with Bhargav Vaidya, 2021

I ran MHD simulations of extragalactic jets with the PLUTO code on an HPC cluster, and injected Lagrangian particles to compute their non-thermal synchrotron emission at different viewing angles. Comparing that emission with observations constrained the simulation parameters. I visualised the results with VisIt and ParaView.

Recent discoveries in magnetar physics

Course project at IIT Bombay with Archana Pai, 2023

A study of what magnetars do, from X-ray activity and short and long bursts to giant flares, quasi-periodic oscillations, glitches and anti-glitches, and of how observations and simulations explain it.

Three-body dynamics in Python

Krittika, IIT Bombay's astronomy club, 2022

I derived the Lagrange points, zero-velocity curves and Poincaré maps of the three-body problem, then simulated particles nudged away from the Lagrange points with the Euler–Richardson method to check that they stay inside the zero-velocity curves.
